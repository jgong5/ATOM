**Found by ART-1 (#157, PR #163) while building the artifact store's keys. Filed so it
outlives that PR. This is a ruling, not a task.**

`07` D41 keys three of the six artifacts on a scalar **`width`**:

| Artifact | Keyed by |
|---|---|
| `price_list` | (model, **width**, source-root digest) |
| `region_terms` | (model, **width**) |
| `memory_readings` | (model, **width**, utilization, max_num_seqs, max_model_len, kv dtype, block size) |

An artifact entry, however, declares a **four-axis topology** — DP, PP, PCP, TP. At
`-tp 2 -dp 2` there are two defensible readings of "width" and **D41 does not say which**:

* **2** — the TP width, which is what every runtime-constant table in `05` is keyed by,
  and what the +5980 MiB collective-buffer step is a function of.
* **4** — the rank count, which is what a per-rank artifact actually has members for.

**Nothing cross-checks them today**, because nothing can: the key field and the topology
field are independent, and an entry keyed `width=2` carrying four ranks' members is
structurally valid.

**ART-1 declined to guess**, which is right — D41's own first incident is *"a bare or
merged price file states no width and silently prices nothing"*, and a store that picks
one reading silently is the same defect one layer up.

### Why it is worth ruling on before the first artifact exists rather than after

The three artifacts above are the campaign's output. A price list written under one
reading and read under the other does not fail: it answers, with a complete-coverage
claim, for a configuration that is not the one asked about. That is the shape of eight
of the incidents D41 lists, and it is cheap to settle now and expensive to settle after
a campaign has run.

`15` D91 already records a neighbouring gap — *"no artifact key currently records which
one"* — so this is the same seam seen from the artifact side.

### What a ruling would have to say

1. Which quantity `width` names in a key: TP width, or rank count.
2. Whether an entry must **refuse** when its topology and its key disagree under that
   definition, or merely record both.
3. Whether the other three axes belong in the key at all, or only in the entry — a
   `price_list` that does not depend on DP is one campaign serving many deployments,
   and one that does is not.

**Not claimable until ruled on.** ART-2 (#161) does not need it; the first real price
list does.
