Split the per-launch constant: plain is stable, collective grows

OpSpec.group already says which launches are collective and TP=1 has
none, so TP=1 identifies the plain constant alone and each higher TP
gives the collective one:

  plain       1.82us/launch
  collective  4.20us at TP=2, 9.72us at TP=4

That is what the hypothesis predicted. The single 2.25us was a blend,
which is why it fitted TP=1 and TP=2 -- where collectives are absent
or a small share -- and broke at TP=4 needing 3.33.

Held out to other models at the same group size the split is a real
but modest improvement: Qwen3-0.6B TP=2 goes +3.71% to +2.11%, and the
30B MoE +10.54% to +9.54%. The MoE is wrong by about the same either
way, so whatever ails it is not the boundary constant.

Two points cannot fix how the collective term scales. 4.20 to 9.72 is
2.3x for a doubled group, which fits linear-in-group, linear in log2,
and nothing cleanly. TP=8 would separate them; until then it is a
measured value per group size rather than a law.
