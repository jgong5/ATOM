Record what the memory budget was made of (item 22, step 1)

--compass-memory-out writes the five device readings the KV sizing is
derived from, and the block counts they produced, per rank. Every run
computes these and discards them once the block count exists, so a run
without the flag is a validation sample that cannot be recovered --
the numbers only exist while the device is in that state.

Taken in get_num_blocks immediately before delegating rather than
after: super() allocates nothing, so these are the same numbers it
will see, while afterwards the KV cache exists and mem_get_info says
something else. Only the raw readings are recorded, never the derived
budget -- that arithmetic is the engine's, is already CPU-only, and
copying it would be one more thing to drift.

Recorded on two configurations:

                  0.6B TP=1   27B TP=2 (per rank)
  total             192.0 GB   192.0 GB
  free              189.5 GB   156.7 GB
  peak_torch          2.6 GB    28.6 GB
  non_torch           0.9 GB     7.0 GB
  cudagraph          0.08 GB    0.08 GB
  -> blocks            95822      37838

First use, and the reason free is recorded separately: both records
can be checked for whether free was the binding term, and in neither
was it -- the utilization budget was, so both are reusable. The 35 GB
between total and free at 27B is this process's own weights and
activations rather than neighbours holding memory, which had to be
computed rather than assumed.
