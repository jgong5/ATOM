Reach a real EP degree, and confirm the comms are still invisible

With the container able to build MORI's kernels and --level 0 avoiding
defect 10, TP=2 x DP=2 with dp-attention runs at ep_size=4. Note
--enforce-eager does not avoid that defect: in ATOM it disables graph
capture, not torch.compile.

Two collectives appear that were absent at ep_size=2, and neither is
expert routing:

  c10d::alltoall_base_  [151936] bf16   vocab-sized, DP's logits exchange
  c10d::allgather_      [2]      int32  token counts across DP

48 moe_forward and not one per-layer collective. Dispatch and combine
are MORI kernels called inside the operator, so they never reach the
dispatcher. The conclusion from ep_size=2 therefore survives being
tested where EP is real: the graph cannot see expert communication at
any EP degree.

Also fixes a silent artifact race found on the way. _rank_coords
reported only tp while _topology reported tp and dp, so under
TP=2 x DP=2 all four ranks resolved to one graph.tp0.json and wrote it
in turn -- three quarters of the evidence discarded, and the surviving
file looked complete. Same defect the artifacts convention was written
to fix, one parallelism later. Every group in _topology now has a
coordinate in _rank_coords.
