Drop empirical/replayed: it was reuse conditions wearing a species name

Reading a budget captured by an earlier run is not a species. The
number is a direct measurement of the quantity, and when it was taken
is not provenance. Every empirical oracle here already works that way:
a price list, the boundary constant, the admission figure and the
calibration table are all measurements written to an artifact and read
back later. If reading from an artifact made a new species they would
all be it.

What I was reaching for was what has to match for the artifact to
remain valid -- ATOM commit, configuration, and free, which on this box
is set by the neighbours. That is an artifact reuse condition, which
was already ruled out as an axis, and I reintroduced it under another
name.

The distinction that is real is whether the lookup matched: exact is
empirical/measured, nearest is empirical/interpolated, past the range
is empirical/extrapolated. That mirrors the time side exactly -- the
price list is exact-match by signature and the interpolated oracle is
nearest-match over step tables -- which is some evidence the scheme is
right.

Step 2 of the memory plan keeps its content and loses the label: read
the artifact instead of the device on an exact key match, and refuse a
key whose free was the binding term.
