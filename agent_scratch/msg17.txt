Trace and price the target model, and find it is a hybrid

Qwen3.8-27B runs now, and it is not the dense transformer this was
scoped around. Its decode graph carries 48
triton::_causal_conv1d_update_kernel and 48
triton::fused_gdn_gating_kernel: 48 layers of gated DeltaNet, linear
attention with a recurrent state, alongside the full-attention ones.

That matters beyond a note. Everything measured on Qwen3-0.6B assumed
attention that grows with context and a KV cache that is walked. A
DeltaNet layer carries fixed-size state and its decode cost does not
grow with history, so the context-interpolation question settled on
the 0.6B -- where four graphs bought little -- was settled on an
architecture that only half applies here.

The first trace also found a hole in the warmup added for exactly
this purpose. Triton autotunes per shape, and the warm pass used
prompts of the same word count rather than the same token count, so
shapes differed and the linear-attention kernels tuned again during
the traced step: 50451 tuning launches in a prefill graph of 51179
operators, ranks disagreeing at 51179 against 50549, coverage 2.7%.
The decode graph was unaffected, which is why it looked fine.

Warming with the same prompts fixes it -- prefill 51179 to 1280, ranks
agreeing, coverage 62.1% -- and needs --no-enable_prefix_caching so
the second pass really re-prefills.

62.1% where the dense 0.6B reaches 98.8%, and the gap is structural.
28 of 37 unpriced signatures fail with "operator not registered in
this process" and every one is triton:: or inductor::. The benchmark
prices by calling torch.ops.<ns>.<op> and a Triton JITFunction is not
one. On the 0.6B that was four signatures; here it is the 48 DeltaNet
layers, 1734 operators, 38% of the graph. Problem 10 was sized S when
Triton kernels were incidental; on the target model it is most of the
linear-attention path.
